Who is the Summit for?

Supply chain professionals at every stage, from students and early-career talent to directors and executives, across pharma, consumer goods, retail, consulting and academia.

What's included in my pass?

Both Summit days, all meals, the DISCOVER Gala, the career fair and workshops. All-Access adds 12 months of membership.

Can I bring a guest to the Gala only?

Yes. Gala guest passes are $49 for people who aren't attending the Summit sessions.

Is there a hotel room block?

Yes. Venue and discounted hotel details are announced with the 2027 venue.

What's the refund policy?

Passes can be transferred to a colleague. Full refund and transfer terms are shown at checkout.
