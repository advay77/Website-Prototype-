Our story

Building bridges. Breaking down barriers.

DISCOVER is a not-for-profit, cross-industry organization that drives the development of leaders in supply chain. Through cross-company collaboration we advance, develop and engage the people who keep the world moving.

MISSION

Develop supply chain leaders

Advance, develop and engage leaders in supply chain functions, adding value to member organizations and the industry as a whole.

WHO WE SERVE

Professionals and students

Professionals seeking networking, development and advancement, and students exploring supply chain careers.

OUR OPPORTUNITY

Change the talent pipeline

Drive sustainable change in the pipeline of supply chain leaders, with diversity at its core.
